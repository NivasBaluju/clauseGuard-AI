1. POSITION TITLE
Texas A&M University System offers you employment as Assistant Director of Compliance in College Station, Texas.

2. SALARY PROVISIONS
Your starting annual rate of pay will be $88,000.00 payable in twelve monthly installments.

3. STATE OF TEXAS BENEFIT PROGRAM
As a State of Texas employee, you are eligible for the Teacher Retirement System (TRS) or Optional Retirement Program (ORP) and state group health insurance.

4. AT-WILL STATUS UNDER TEXAS LAW
Employment is at-will pursuant to the laws of the State of Texas and policies of the Texas A&M University System Board of Regents.

5. SYSTEM IP REGULATIONS
All inventions and discoverable IP developed using System resources are subject to TAMUS System Regulation 17.01.01.

6. VERIFICATION PREREQUISITES
Employment is conditioned upon an acceptable criminal history background check and Form I-9 verification.

7. ACCEPTANCE DEADLINE
We request your formal response within seven (7) business days.

8. WORKING HOURS AND SCHEDULE
Normal working hours are 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday, totaling 40 hours per week at the System headquarters in College Station.

9. ANNUAL MERIT INCENTIVE ELIGIBILITY
You may be eligible for annual merit salary adjustments subject to legislative appropriations and satisfactory annual performance evaluation ratings.

10. ETHICS AND CONFIDENTIALITY
You are subject to Texas government ethics laws and agree to maintain the strict confidentiality of all official compliance investigations and proprietary data.

11. CONFLICT OF INTEREST POLICY
You agree to adhere to TAMUS System Regulation 31.05.01 regarding outside employment and conflict of interest restrictions.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GRIEVANCE
Disputes regarding employment conditions shall be resolved through the TAMUS Staff Grievance Procedure (Regulation 32.01.01).

13. SEPARATION CONDITIONS
Employment is at-will. A minimum of two (2) weeks advance notice is requested upon voluntary separation.
