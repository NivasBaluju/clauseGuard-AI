1. POSITION ASSIGNMENT
Offer of employment as Cyber Threat Intelligence Analyst with the Department of Information Resources in Austin, TX.

2. SALARY PROVISIONS
Annual salary of $92,000.00 paid semi-monthly.

3. EMPLOYEES RETIREMENT SYSTEM OF TEXAS (ERS)
Coverage under ERS pension program and Texas Employees Group Benefits Program (GBP).

4. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
Employment with the State of Texas is at-will and can be terminated at any time by either party.

5. SECURITY CLEARANCE CONTINGENCY
Contingent upon FBI CJIS fingerprint background clearance and E-Verify authorization.

6. RESPONSE WINDOW
Please accept by July 18, 2024.

7. WORK HOURS AND LOCATION
Standard working hours are 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Monday through Friday in Austin, TX, with telework eligibility.

8. MERIT PAY ELIGIBILITY
Eligible for state merit salary increases and one-time merit awards pursuant to Texas Government Code Section 659.255.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY AND CJIS SECURITY
You agree to maintain the strict confidentiality of all state cybersecurity intelligence and threat analyses.

10. OUTSIDE EMPLOYMENT ETHICS
Must obtain written approval prior to engaging in any outside employment per DIR Ethics Policy.

11. INTERNAL GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
Employment concerns may be addressed through the DIR Employee Dispute Resolution Procedure.

12. VOLUNTARY RESIGNATION NOTICE
A minimum of two (2) weeks advance written notice of resignation is requested.
