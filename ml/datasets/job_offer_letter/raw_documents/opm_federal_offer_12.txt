1. FEDERAL CIVIL SERVICE APPOINTMENT
Formal offer of career-conditional appointment to the position of Program Analyst, GS-0343-13, Step 1, with the General Services Administration.

2. FEDERAL GENERAL SCHEDULE PAY
Starting salary of $112,015.00 per annum (including locality pay for Washington-Baltimore-Arlington, DC-MD-VA).

3. FERS AND FEHB ENROLLMENT
Participation in Federal Employees Retirement System (FERS), Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) with 5% government match, and Federal Employees Health Benefits (FEHB).

4. PROBATIONARY PERIOD AND MERIT SYSTEM
Subject to a one-year probationary period under Title 5 of the United States Code.

5. SECURITY CLEARANCE CONTINGENCY
Contingent on favorable adjudication of a Secret security clearance via SF-86 background investigation.

6. REPORTING DATE
Anticipated entrance-on-duty date is August 26, 2024.

7. TOUR OF DUTY AND WORK SCHEDULE
Standard 80-hour bi-weekly tour of duty with flexible working hours between 7:00 AM and 6:00 PM pursuant to GSA alternative work schedules.

8. PERFORMANCE AWARDS AND WITHIN-GRADE INCREASES
Eligible for annual performance-based cash awards and statutory Within-Grade Increases (WGI) under 5 CFR Part 531.

9. STANDARDS OF ETHICAL CONDUCT
Subject to Title 5 CFR Part 2635 Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch and conflict of interest restrictions.

10. PRIVACY ACT AND NON-DISCLOSURE
You are bound by the Federal Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a) to protect sensitive procurement and agency records.

11. ADMINISTRATIVE GRIEVANCE SYSTEM
Disputes may be pursued through the agency Administrative Grievance Procedure or Merit Systems Protection Board (MSPB).
