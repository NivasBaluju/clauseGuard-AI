1. TITLE AND COMPENSATION
University of Washington offers you the position of Senior Cloud Architect at an annual salary of $145,000.00 in Seattle, WA.

2. RETIREMENT AND MEDICAL BENEFITS
Eligible for the UW Retirement Plan (UWRP) or PERS, Public Employees Benefits Board (PEBB) medical, dental, and life insurance.

3. VACATION ACCRUAL
Staff accrue vacation leave at 12 hours per month with standard annual roll-over caps.

4. PATENT ASSIGNMENT POLICY
All inventions and software source code created within your scope of employment belong to the University of Washington.

5. PRE-EMPLOYMENT CONTINGENCY
Offer is contingent upon sexual misconduct disclosure checks per Washington RCW 28B.112.080 and criminal background screening.

6. EXPIRATION DATE
Offer expires five (5) business days after receipt.

7. WORKING HOURS AND HYBRID ARRANGEMENT
This position requires standard core working hours of 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM with approved telework pursuant to UW Telework Agreement guidelines.

8. MERIT INCENTIVE AND SALARY REVIEW
Staff salaries are reviewed annually for merit adjustments in accordance with university compensation policies and legislative funding.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY OF SENSITIVE RECORDS
You agree to maintain confidentiality of proprietary computing architectures, medical data, and student records pursuant to UW Administrative Policy Statements.

10. CONFLICT OF INTEREST
You must disclose outside professional work and comply with Washington State Ethics in Public Service Act (RCW 42.52).

11. GRIEVANCE AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Employment concerns may be addressed through the University of Washington Staff Complaint and Grievance Procedure.

12. SEPARATION NOTICE
Staff are requested to provide thirty (30) days written notice prior to voluntary separation.
