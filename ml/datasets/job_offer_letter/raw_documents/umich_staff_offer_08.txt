1. TITLE AND DUTIES
Offer of employment as Application Developer Senior within Information and Technology Services (ITS) in Ann Arbor, MI.

2. ANNUAL SALARY AND MERIT REVIEW
Your annual base salary will be $118,000.00, reviewed annually for merit adjustments.

3. TIAA RETIREMENT MATCHING
Eligible for 2-for-1 university matching contributions (up to 10%) in the basic retirement plan after one year.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY BYLAW 3.10
Inventions and patents created by employees are governed by University of Michigan Bylaw 3.10.

5. CONTINGENCY ON BACKGROUND
Contingent on a satisfactory background check and legal authorization to work.

6. DEADLINE FOR ACCEPTANCE
Valid until August 1, 2024.

7. WORK HOURS AND SCHEDULE
Standard 40-hour work week with core hours 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Remote work eligibility subject to departmental telecommuting agreement.

8. MERIT SALARY ADJUSTMENT
Salaries are reviewed annually for merit adjustments as part of the University annual compensation review cycle.

9. CONFIDENTIAL DATA PROTECTION
You agree to safeguard proprietary ITS infrastructure data, health information, and student records under Michigan Standard Practice Guide 601.12.

10. OUTSIDE ACTIVITIES AND CONFLICT OF INTEREST
You must disclose outside commercial consulting and comply with SPG 201.65-1 Regarding Conflicts of Interest.

11. GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION PROCEDURE
Employment issues may be submitted to the University of Michigan Staff Grievance Procedure under SPG 201.08.

12. SEPARATION AND RESIGNATION
Two (2) weeks written advance notice is requested upon voluntary resignation.
