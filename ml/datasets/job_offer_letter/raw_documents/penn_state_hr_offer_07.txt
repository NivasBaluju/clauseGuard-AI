1. POSITION AND START DATE
Penn State offers you the position of Financial Analyst in the Office of the Corporate Controller starting August 12, 2024.

2. ANNUAL SALARY
Your starting salary is $82,000.00 paid bi-weekly.

3. HEALTH AND RETIREMENT
Participation in SERS or TIAA retirement plans, Highmark medical plans, and tuition discount benefits.

4. NON-DISCLOSURE OF PROPRIETARY DATA
You agree to protect confidential student and financial data in accordance with university policy AD95.

5. CRIMINAL AND EDUCATION SCREENING
Appointment is contingent upon successful completion of background checks per Pennsylvania Act 153.

6. ACCEPTANCE TIMEFRAME
Please return signed acceptance by July 25, 2024.

7. HOURS OF WORK AND TELECOMMUTING
Standard hours are 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday (40 hours/week) in the Corporate Controller's Office with hybrid flexibility.

8. ANNUAL MERIT COMPENSATION
You will be eligible for annual merit salary review based on performance achievement against agreed objectives.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY POLICY IP01
Inventions and discoveries made using university funds or facilities are subject to Penn State Policy IP01.

10. CONFLICT OF COMMITMENT
You agree to disclose outside consulting and refrain from activities that conflict with university business per Policy HR42.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Employment disputes may be resolved through the Penn State Staff Grievance Policy HR76.

12. RESIGNATION NOTICE
A minimum of two weeks written notice is requested for professional separations.
