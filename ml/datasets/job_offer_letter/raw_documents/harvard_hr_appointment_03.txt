1. APPOINTMENT SPECIFICATION
We are delighted to offer you an administrative appointment as Program Manager within the Faculty of Arts and Sciences, reporting to the Executive Director.

2. SALARY TERMS
Your starting annual compensation will be $112,000.00, paid semi-monthly according to Harvard's standard administrative payroll schedule.

3. UNIVERSITY BENEFITS ENROLLMENT
You will be eligible for Harvard's retirement plans, health insurance (Harvard University Health Services / BCBS), dental plans, and tuition assistance programs.

4. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT RELATIONSHIP
Your appointment is at-will. Either you or Harvard may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice.

5. CONFLICT OF INTEREST AND NON-SOLICITATION
During your employment, you agree not to engage in outside professional activities that conflict with Harvard's mission. For twelve (12) months post-termination, you agree not to solicit staff members to leave university employment.

6. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND HARVARD IP
You acknowledge that all research data, administrative files, and intellectual work created during your employment remain the exclusive property of President and Fellows of Harvard College.

7. CONTINGENCY ON BACKGROUND VERIFICATION
Employment is contingent upon successful completion of a reference check and standard background verification.

8. ACCEPTANCE WINDOW
Please sign and return this appointment letter within two weeks of receipt.
