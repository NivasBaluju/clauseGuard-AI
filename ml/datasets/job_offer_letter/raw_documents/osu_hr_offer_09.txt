1. ROLE AND DEPARTMENT
Offer of appointment as Senior Project Coordinator in the Wexner Medical Center at Ohio State University.

2. BASE SALARY
Starting salary of $78,000.00 annually, paid on a monthly basis.

3. OPERS / STRS RETIREMENT
Mandatory participation in Ohio Public Employees Retirement System (OPERS) or Alternative Retirement Plan (ARP).

4. EMPLOYMENT STATUS
Employment is unclassified and serves at the discretion of the university trustees.

5. DRUG SCREEN AND CONTINGENCIES
Contingent on hospital compliance, background investigation, and mandatory drug screening.

6. RESPONSE DATE
Please respond within 10 days of letter issuance.

7. SCHEDULE AND WORKPLACE LOCATION
Full-time 40-hour position at Wexner Medical Center with scheduled shifts from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

8. MERIT INCENTIVE ADJUSTMENTS
Eligible for annual university merit compensation increases based on fiscal year-end performance appraisals.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY AND HIPAA SECURITY
You agree to protect patient health records and confidential hospital data under Ohio State Wexner Medical Center compliance policies.

10. ETHICS AND CONFLICT RESTRICTION
Subject to the Ohio Ethics Law (RC Chapter 102) prohibiting private conflicts of interest in public university employment.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Workplace grievances are handled pursuant to Ohio State Human Resources Policy 8.05.

12. RESIGNATION NOTICE
A minimum of two weeks written notice of resignation is required.
