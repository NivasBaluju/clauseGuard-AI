SECTION 1: APPOINTMENT AND ROLE
We are pleased to offer you the career appointment position of Senior Systems Analyst with the University of California Office of the President, reporting to the Director of Enterprise Architecture. Your primary work location will be the Oakland headquarters, with eligibility for hybrid telecommuting pursuant to university policy.

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION AND SALARY SCHEDULE
Your annual starting salary will be $128,000.00, paid on a monthly basis in accordance with the university staff personnel payroll schedule, subject to standard federal and California tax withholdings.

SECTION 3: RETIREMENT AND HEALTH BENEFITS
You will be eligible to participate in the University of California Retirement Plan (UCRP), comprehensive health, vision, and dental insurance, disability coverage, and university life insurance plans effective the first day of the month following your hire date.

SECTION 4: VACATION AND PAID LEAVE
You will accrue paid vacation at the rate of 10 hours per month (15 days annually) and paid sick leave at 8 hours per month, in addition to 14 paid university holidays per academic calendar year.

SECTION 5: AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
As a non-represented staff member in the Professional and Support Staff (PSS) program, your employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party with or without cause and with or without advance notice.

SECTION 6: PATENT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
As a condition of employment, you must sign the State of California Oath of Allegiance and the standard University of California Patent Agreement, assigning to the university inventions and intellectual property conceived during university employment using university facilities.

SECTION 7: BACKGROUND AND RIGHT-TO-WORK CONTINGENCIES
This offer is contingent upon satisfactory completion of a background check including fingerprinting, and your ability to furnish documentation verifying legal authorization to work in the United States on Form I-9 within three (3) business days of your first day of employment.

SECTION 8: OFFER ACCEPTANCE DEADLINE
Please confirm your acceptance of this offer by signing and returning this letter by 5:00 PM Pacific Time on July 15, 2024, after which this offer will expire.
