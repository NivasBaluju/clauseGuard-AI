1. CIVIL SERVICE CLASSIFICATION
Conditional job offer for the civil service classification of Information Technology Specialist I with the Department of Technology in Sacramento, CA.

2. MONTHLY SALARY RANGE
Your starting compensation will be Range C at $7,650.00 per month pursuant to California civil service pay scales.

3. CALPERS RETIREMENT
Enrollment in California Public Employees' Retirement System (CalPERS) defined benefit pension program.

4. PROBATIONARY PERIOD
You must successfully serve a twelve (12) month probationary period before acquiring permanent civil service tenure.

5. LIVE SCAN CONTINGENCY
Contingent upon DOJ/FBI fingerprint Live Scan clearance and proof of citizenship or legal residence.

6. EXPIRATION OF OFFER
This offer must be accepted within seven (7) business days.

7. WORK SCHEDULE AND CORE HOURS
Standard 40-hour work schedule, Monday through Friday 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM at Department of Technology headquarters.

8. MERIT SALARY ADJUSTMENT (MSA)
Eligible for annual Merit Salary Adjustments of 5% up to maximum of civil service salary range upon satisfactory service.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY OF STATE IT ASSETS
You agree to comply with State Administrative Manual (SAM) security guidelines and safeguard confidential government databases.

10. INCOMPATIBLE ACTIVITIES STATEMENT
You must execute the Department's Statement of Incompatible Activities prohibiting outside employment that conflicts with state duties.

11. MERIT SYSTEM GRIEVANCE AND APPEAL
Employment disputes are governed by California Government Code Title 2 and State Personnel Board appeals.
