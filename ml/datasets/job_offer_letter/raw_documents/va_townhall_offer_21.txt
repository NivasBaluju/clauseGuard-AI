1. EXECUTIVE POSITION
Offer of appointment as Deputy Executive Director in Richmond, VA.

2. SALARY AND PERFORMANCE BONUS
Base salary of $150,000.00 plus discretionary incentive bonus up to 15%.

3. RETIREMENT PLAN
Coverage under VRS Hybrid Retirement Plan.

4. NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICIT (AGGRESSIVE)
Employee agrees for 24 months post-termination not to engage in competing consulting or solicit agency staff throughout the Commonwealth of Virginia.

5. BINDING ARBITRATION
All employment claims must be resolved by binding AAA arbitration with waiver of court trial.

6. CONTINGENCY
Subject to executive background check and financial disclosure statements.

7. CORE OFFICE HOURS AND EXECUTIVE SCHEDULE
Standard hours are 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM at agency headquarters with regular executive availability expected.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY OF EXECUTIVE PROCEEDINGS
You agree to hold confidential all non-public regulatory proceedings and executive agency deliberations.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
All official publications, white papers, and regulatory methodologies prepared belong to the Commonwealth.

10. SEVERANCE AND RESIGNATION SCHEDULE
In the event of separation without cause, executive will receive 3 months severance pay subject to release.
