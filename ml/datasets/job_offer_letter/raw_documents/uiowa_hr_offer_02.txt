1. POSITION TITLE AND DEPARTMENT
The University of Iowa is pleased to offer you the position of Research Data Scientist in the Department of Biostatistics, College of Public Health.

2. COMPENSATION AND PAY PERIODS
Your annual base salary will be $98,500.00, paid on the last working day of each month via direct deposit.

3. PERFORMANCE INCENTIVE PLAN
You may be eligible for annual merit salary adjustments and performance bonuses subject to annual collegiate performance evaluations and Board of Regents funding appropriations.

4. BENEFIT PACKAGE OVERVIEW
You are eligible for participation in TIAA or IPERS retirement programs with generous university contributions, comprehensive medical insurance through UI Select or UI Choice, dental, and disability coverage.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND FERPA/HIPAA
You agree to maintain strict confidentiality of all student educational records and patient protected health information (PHI) in compliance with FERPA, HIPAA, and university data governance regulations.

6. UNIVERSITY INVENTIONS ASSIGNMENT
You agree to assign all rights in patents, copyrights, and intellectual property developed in the course of your sponsored research employment to the University of Iowa Research Foundation.

7. PRE-EMPLOYMENT CONTINGENCIES
This appointment is contingent upon successful verification of criminal background screening and educational credentials.

8. RESPONSE DEADLINE
This offer remains open for ten (10) calendar days from the date of this letter.
