1. PARTIES AND APARTMENT
Lease between Cayuga Heights Rentals ('Landlord') and Cornell Student ('Tenant') for premises at 204 Dryden Road, Ithaca, NY 14850.

2. RENT AND LATE CHARGE STATUTORY CAP
Rent is $1,750.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. Pursuant to New York Real Property Law Section 238-a, late fees are capped at fifty dollars ($50.00) or five percent (5%) of monthly rent, whichever is less, after a 5-day grace period.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT CAP AND TRUST ACCOUNTING
Under NY General Obligations Law 7-103, deposit of $1,750.00 (maximum one month's rent) is held in an interest-bearing trust account at Tompkins Community Bank. The deposit shall be returned within fourteen (14) days after Tenant vacates with an itemized accounting.

4. LANDLORD WARRANTY OF HABITABILITY
Landlord warrants that the apartment is fit for human habitation under NY Real Property Law Section 235-b. Landlord shall provide adequate heat between October 1 and May 31 (minimum 68 degrees during the day).

5. ENTRY NOTICE COVENANT
Landlord must give at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice in writing or by email prior to non-emergency entry. Entry shall only occur on weekdays during customary business hours.

6. STATUTORY RIGHT TO SUBLET
Pursuant to NY Real Property Law Section 226-b, Tenant has the statutory right to sublet upon written request to Landlord accompanied by subtenant details. Landlord cannot unreasonably refuse consent.

7. PET RULES AND PROHIBITIONS
Pets are prohibited unless authorized by a written rider. Service and support animals are accommodated in accordance with the New York State Human Rights Law.

8. TERMINATION AND NOTICE TO QUIT
Notice of non-renewal or intent to terminate month-to-month tenancy must be provided at least thirty (30) days in advance.

9. LATE FEES (RPL 238-a)
Pursuant to NY Real Property Law Section 238-a, late fees are capped at $50.00 or 5% of rent after a 5-day grace period.

10. ALTERATIONS AND PAINTING
Tenant shall make no modifications or alterations to walls or structural components without Landlord's written consent.

11. RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is strongly advised to maintain personal property and liability insurance up to $100,000.00.

12. INDEMNITY ALLOCATION
Tenant indemnifies Landlord against liability resulting from Tenant's negligence or intentional misconduct.

13. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
Tenant pays NYSEG electric and gas. Landlord pays City of Ithaca water, sewer, and regular refuse collection.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Parties may submit disputes to mediation through the Community Dispute Resolution Center of Tompkins County.

15. AUTOMATIC EXTENSION
Lease term ends July 31, 2025. Any renewal must be agreed upon in writing at least 60 days before expiration.
