CLAUSE 1: RENT PAYMENT TERMS
Rent is $1,550.00 per month, due on the first of each month. A late fee of $40.00 applies after the 5th calendar day.

CLAUSE 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT LIMITS (68 P.S. 250.511a)
Deposit of $1,550.00 (limited to two months rent in first year, one month in second year) is held in an escrow account. Landlord shall provide itemized damages list and return deposit within thirty (30) days.

CLAUSE 3: IMPLIED WARRANTY OF HABITABILITY
Landlord warrants that the dwelling unit is fit for human habitation as recognized in Pugh v. Holmes. Landlord maintains essential heat, water, and sanitary facilities.

CLAUSE 4: ENTRY NOTICE COVENANT
Landlord must provide at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice prior to entering for non-emergency inspections.

CLAUSE 5: SUBLETTING TERMS
No sublease is valid without prior written consent of Landlord.

CLAUSE 6: TERMINATION PROCEDURES
Thirty (30) days written notice is required to terminate or non-renew at lease expiration.

CLAUSE 7: LATE FEE RESTRICTIONS
Late fee of $40.00 applies after the 5th calendar day if rent remains unpaid.

CLAUSE 8: ALTERATIONS AND FIXTURES
Tenant shall make no physical modifications, wall painting, or lock alterations without Landlord's written approval.

CLAUSE 9: RENTER'S LIABILITY INSURANCE
Tenant is advised to maintain personal property and premises liability insurance up to $100,000.00.

CLAUSE 10: INDEMNITY
Tenant agrees to indemnify Landlord from damages caused by Tenant's negligence or violation of lease terms.

CLAUSE 11: UTILITY RESPONSIBILITY
Tenant pays electric and gas. Landlord provides municipal water, sewer, and regular trash removal.

CLAUSE 12: DISPUTE MEDIATION
Parties may participate in community mediation services prior to commencing legal action in district court.

CLAUSE 13: AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Converts to month-to-month tenancy upon term expiration unless thirty (30) days written notice of termination is served.

CLAUSE 14: PET RULES
Pets allowed with written consent and $250.00 pet deposit. Service animals are permitted in compliance with fair housing laws.

CLAUSE 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the Pennsylvania Landlord and Tenant Act of 1951 (68 P.S. Section 250.101 et seq.).
