SECTION 1: RENT PAYMENT
Rent is $1,650.00 per month, due on the first day of each calendar month. In accordance with Illinois law, late fees cannot exceed $20.00 or 20% of monthly rent and may only apply after a full 5-day grace period.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW (765 ILCS 710)
Tenant deposits $1,650.00. Under Illinois Security Deposit Return Act, Landlord must provide an itemized list of damages within thirty (30) days and return the deposit balance within forty-five (45) days.

SECTION 3: HABITABILITY AND HEATING
Landlord warrants compliance with municipal housing maintenance codes and promises to furnish heating to a minimum temperature of 68 degrees during the heating season (September 15 through June 1).

SECTION 4: LANDLORD RIGHT OF ENTRY
Landlord shall provide at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice before entering to inspect or show the unit, except in case of sudden water or gas leak.

SECTION 5: SUBLEASING RIGHTS
Under Chicago Residential Landlord Tenant Ordinance (RLTO), Tenant has the absolute right to sublease without paying excessive fees, and Landlord shall accept reasonable subtenants.

SECTION 6: PET RESTRICTIONS AND FEES
Pets require written permission and a $200.00 refundable pet deposit. Certified service animals are exempt from all pet restrictions.

SECTION 7: TERMINATION NOTICE REQUIREMENT
Thirty (30) days written notice is required to terminate a month-to-month tenancy or non-renew at lease expiration.

SECTION 8: LATE FEES AND STATUTORY CEILING
Late fee is $20.00, applicable only if rent remains unpaid after a 5-day grace period, pursuant to Illinois statutory guidelines.

SECTION 9: ALTERATIONS AND IMPROVEMENTS
Tenant shall make no structural alterations, painting, or lock alterations without Landlord's written authorization.

SECTION 10: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is advised to maintain personal property and premises liability coverage of $100,000.00.

SECTION 11: INDEMNIFICATION RESTRICTIONS
Tenant agrees to indemnify Landlord solely for losses resulting directly from Tenant's own negligent acts.

SECTION 12: UTILITIES RESPONSIBILITY
Tenant pays separately metered electric and gas. Landlord pays city water and common refuse disposal.

SECTION 13: DISPUTE MEDIATION
Parties agree to participate in the Center for Conflict Resolution mediation program before court eviction filings.

SECTION 14: LEASE RENEWAL
Month-to-month extension requires thirty (30) days written notice of non-renewal by either party.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of the State of Illinois and Chicago Residential Landlord and Tenant Ordinance where applicable.
