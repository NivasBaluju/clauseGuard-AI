SECTION 1: RENT PAYMENT AND FOUR-DAY GRACE PERIOD (ORS 90.260)
Rent is $1,700.00 per month. Under Oregon law, a late fee may only be charged if rent is unpaid after the fourth day of the rental period. Late fee is fixed at $50.00.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT TRUST ACCOUNT (ORS 90.300)
Security deposit of $1,700.00 is deposited in an Oregon bank account. Landlord shall provide a written accounting and refund within thirty-one (31) days after termination.

SECTION 3: LANDLORD HABITABILITY OBLIGATION (ORS 90.320)
Landlord covenants that premises are habitable, including effective weatherproofing, plumbing, heating facilities, and hot water systems.

SECTION 4: 24-HOUR ENTRY NOTICE (ORS 90.322)
Landlord may enter premises only after giving at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice in writing, specifying date and time during reasonable hours.

SECTION 5: SUBLEASING AND ROOMMATES
Tenant shall not sublease without prior written permission. Sublease requests will be reviewed under standard criteria.

SECTION 6: TERMINATION RESTRICTIONS (ORS 90.427)
Termination of month-to-month tenancies after the first year requires at least 90 days written notice and must be based on statutory landlord cause.

SECTION 7: LATE CHARGES (ORS 90.260)
Late fee is $50.00, applicable only after the fourth (4th) day of the rental period.

SECTION 8: PREMISES ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall make no structural changes, wall painting, or re-keying without prior written consent.

SECTION 9: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is advised to secure renter's personal property and liability insurance coverage.

SECTION 10: INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant agrees to hold Landlord harmless against claims arising from Tenant's own negligence.

SECTION 11: UTILITIES ALLOCATION
Tenant pays electric and gas. Landlord pays municipal water, sewer, and curbside recycling.

SECTION 12: DISPUTE MEDIATION
Parties may submit controversies to community dispute resolution programs under ORS 36.100.

SECTION 13: LEASE EXTENSION
Converts to month-to-month tenancy upon term expiration under Oregon landlord-tenant laws.

SECTION 14: PET POLICIES
Pets require written authorization and $250.00 pet deposit. Assistance animals exempt under fair housing laws.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the Oregon Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (ORS Chapter 90).
