CLAUSE 1: RENT PAYMENT
The rent for the premises is $1,900.00 per month, due on the first day of each month. In accordance with Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 186 Section 15B, no late fee may be assessed unless rent is unpaid for thirty (30) full days after the due date.

CLAUSE 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW AND INTEREST
Landlord acknowledges receipt of $1,900.00 to be held in an interest-bearing escrow account at Bank of America. Landlord shall pay 5% interest annually or the actual bank interest rate to Tenant. Deposit will be returned within thirty (30) days with an itemized statement of repairs sworn under penalties of perjury.

CLAUSE 3: CONDITION OF PREMISES STATEMENT
Landlord provides Tenant with a written Statement of Condition detailing existing defects within ten (10) days of the commencement of tenancy. Landlord warrants full compliance with the Massachusetts State Sanitary Code Chapter II.

CLAUSE 4: LANDLORD RIGHT OF ACCESS
Landlord may enter the premises only upon reasonable advance notice of at least twenty-four (24) hours to inspect, make repairs, or exhibit the premises pursuant to M.G.L. c. 186 Section 15B. Unannounced entry is prohibited.

CLAUSE 5: PROHIBITION ON EXCULPATORY CLAUSES
Any provision seeking to relieve Landlord of liability for injuries or damages caused by Landlord's negligence or failure to maintain the premises is void as against Massachusetts public policy.

CLAUSE 6: SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT
Tenant may sublet the premises with the Landlord's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed. Any replacement tenant meeting reasonable financial criteria shall be accepted.

CLAUSE 7: PET COVENANT
No dogs or cats shall be maintained without written consent. Certified assistance animals required for medical or psychological reasons are accepted without fee or deposit.

CLAUSE 8: TERMINATION BY STATUTORY NOTICE
Tenant or Landlord may terminate this month-to-month tenancy by serving written notice of at least thirty (30) days or one full rental period, whichever is longer, conforming to M.G.L. c. 186 Section 12.

CLAUSE 9: JURISDICTION
This agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Any actions shall be brought in the Housing Court Department of Massachusetts.

CLAUSE 10: LATE FEE LIMITATIONS (M.G.L. c. 186 s. 15B)
No late fee or penalty may be assessed unless rent remains unpaid for thirty (30) full days after the due date. Any late fee assessed after 30 days shall not exceed $35.00.

CLAUSE 11: ALTERATIONS AND PAINTING
Tenant shall make no structural alterations, painting, or wallpapering without prior written consent of Landlord.

CLAUSE 12: TENANT PROPERTY INSURANCE
Landlord's property insurance does not insure Tenant's personal belongings against theft, fire, or water damage. Tenant is advised to procure renter's insurance.

CLAUSE 13: UTILITIES ALLOCATION
Tenant pays separately metered electricity and heating gas. Landlord pays cold water, municipal sewerage, and trash collection pursuant to the State Sanitary Code.

CLAUSE 14: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Parties may submit controversies to mediation through the Massachusetts Housing Court Alternative Dispute Resolution Department.

CLAUSE 15: MONTH-TO-MONTH EXTENSION
Upon term expiration, this agreement converts to a month-to-month tenancy terminable by thirty (30) days written notice.
