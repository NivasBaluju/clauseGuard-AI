SECTION 1: RENT PAYMENT
Rent is $1,450.00 per month, due on the first day of each month. A late fee of $50.00 shall be assessed if rent is not received by the 5th.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW (OCGA 44-7-31)
Tenant deposits $1,450.00 held in an escrow account at a state-regulated bank. Landlord will inspect premises within three days of surrender and return deposit within thirty (30) days.

SECTION 3: REPAIR AND MAINTENANCE OBLIGATIONS
Landlord shall keep the premises in repair and is liable for damages arising from failure to maintain structural, plumbing, and electrical systems.

SECTION 4: LANDLORD RIGHT OF ACCESS
Landlord may enter premises with reasonable advance notice of at least 24 hours for inspection and repair.

SECTION 5: SUBLEASING RESTRICTIONS
Tenant shall not sublease or assign the apartment without prior written permission of Landlord.

SECTION 6: TERMINATION NOTICES (OCGA 44-7-7)
Landlord must provide sixty (60) days notice to terminate a tenancy-at-will; Tenant must provide thirty (30) days notice.

SECTION 7: LATE CHARGE PROVISIONS
Late fee of $50.00 applies if rent is not received by the 5th day of the month.

SECTION 8: PROPERTY ALTERATIONS
No alterations, painting, or structural changes without Landlord's prior written permission.

SECTION 9: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is encouraged to maintain renter's insurance with $100,000.00 liability protection.

SECTION 10: INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant indemnifies Landlord against claims caused solely by Tenant's own negligence on the premises.

SECTION 11: UTILITIES PAYMENT
Tenant pays electric and gas. Landlord provides municipal water, sewer, and garbage collection.

SECTION 12: DISPUTE MEDIATION
Parties may submit tenancy disputes to Georgia Office of Dispute Resolution programs.

SECTION 13: AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Lease converts to tenancy-at-will with sixty (60) days notice required by Landlord or thirty (30) days by Tenant.

SECTION 14: PET RESTRICTIONS
Pets require written authorization and $250.00 deposit. Service animals accommodated without charge.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of the State of Georgia and OCGA Title 44 Chapter 7.
