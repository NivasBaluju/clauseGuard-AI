SECTION 1: RENTAL AMOUNT AND MAXIMUM LATE FEES
Rent is $1,750.00 per month. Under Maryland Real Property Code Section 8-208, late fees are capped at five percent (5%) of monthly rent and can only be assessed after rent is 5 days late.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT CEILING (TWO MONTHS MAXIMUM)
Security deposit is $1,750.00. Landlord shall deposit funds in an insured financial institution within thirty (30) days. Under Real Property Section 8-203, Landlord must return deposit with statutory interest within forty-five (45) days of moving out.

SECTION 3: LANDLORD MAINTENANCE COVENANT
Landlord covenants to keep premises in clean, safe condition, free from rodent infestation, and maintain heating facilities capable of 68 degrees.

SECTION 4: RIGHT OF ENTRY (24 HOURS NOTICE)
Except for emergency situations, Landlord shall provide at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice before entering for non-emergency repairs.

SECTION 5: SUBLEASING CONDITIONS
Tenant shall not assign this lease or sublet the premises without Landlord's written permission, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

SECTION 6: SERVICE ANIMALS AND PET POLICY
Pets require written authorization. Legally recognized assistance animals are accommodated without pet fees or deposits.

SECTION 7: TERMINATION AND NOTICE TO VACATE
Either party may terminate month-to-month tenancy by serving written notice of at least sixty (60) days under Maryland Real Property Code Section 8-402.

SECTION 8: LATE CHARGES (MD REAL PROPERTY 8-208)
Late fee is capped at 5% of monthly rent and may only be charged after rent is 5 full days delinquent.

SECTION 9: PROPERTY ALTERATIONS
No alterations, decorating, or wallpapering without Landlord's written permission.

SECTION 10: RENTER'S LIABILITY INSURANCE
Tenant is advised to maintain insurance covering personal effects and liability up to $100,000.00.

SECTION 11: INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant indemnifies Landlord against claims caused solely by Tenant's own negligence on premises.

SECTION 12: UTILITY SERVICES
Tenant pays electric and gas. Landlord provides water and regular trash removal.

SECTION 13: MEDIATION
Parties agree to seek mediation through the Maryland Community Mediation Center before litigation.

SECTION 14: LEASE RENEWAL
Sixty (60) days advance notice is required to terminate or non-renew at term expiration under Maryland law.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by Maryland Real Property Code Title 8.
