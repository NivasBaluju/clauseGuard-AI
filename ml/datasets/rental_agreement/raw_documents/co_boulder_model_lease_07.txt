SECTION 1: TERM AND RENT
Tenancy commences August 1, 2024, and ends July 31, 2025. Rent is $1,800.00 per month, payable by electronic transfer on the 1st of each month. A grace period extends through the 7th of the month, after which a $35.00 late fee applies.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT AND CITY INTEREST ORDINANCE
Tenant deposits $1,800.00 with Landlord. Under City of Boulder Revised Code 12-2, Landlord shall pay statutory interest annually on the deposit. The deposit shall be returned within thirty (30) days following tenancy termination.

SECTION 3: HABITABILITY MAINTENANCE
Landlord shall maintain heating appliances capable of maintaining 68 degrees Fahrenheit, weatherproofing, and plumbing in accordance with the Colorado Warranty of Habitability Act (C.R.S. 38-12-503).

SECTION 4: ADVANCE NOTICE FOR ENTRY
Landlord or agents must provide at least forty-eight (48) hours advance written notice before entering for inspections or non-emergency repairs, specifying the two-hour entry window.

SECTION 5: SUBLEASING AND ROOMMATES
Tenant has the right to sublease to any prospective subtenant who meets standard credit criteria upon giving Landlord 14 days notice. Landlord shall not charge an administrative subletting fee in excess of $50.00.

SECTION 6: PET RULES
Pets are permitted subject to a $250.00 refundable pet deposit. Under Colorado law, no breed-specific bans shall apply to service or companion animals.

SECTION 7: UTILITIES AND RECYCLING
Landlord provides trash, municipal recycling, and compost collection. Tenant establishes accounts for gas, electricity, and telecommunications.

SECTION 8: DISPUTE MEDIATION
The parties agree to participate in the City of Boulder Community Mediation Service to resolve any landlord-tenant disputes before filing an action in county court.

SECTION 9: NOTICE OF TERMINATION
Either party may terminate the lease at the end of the term by giving at least sixty (60) days advance written notice.

SECTION 10: LATE FEES AND BOULDER ORDINANCE
If rent is not paid by the 7th of the month, a reasonable administrative fee of $35.00 shall be assessed. No daily compounding charges shall apply.

SECTION 11: PREMISES ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall not paint walls, drill into masonry, or install permanent fixtures without advance written permission from Landlord.

SECTION 12: TENANT INSURANCE
Tenant is encouraged to maintain renter's insurance covering personal property and premises liability up to $100,000.00.

SECTION 13: INDEMNIFICATION RESTRICTIONS
Tenant indemnifies Landlord against liabilities resulting directly from the negligent acts or omissions of Tenant or Tenant's invitees.

SECTION 14: AUTOMATIC RENEWAL TERMS
This lease shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis at the end of the term unless sixty (60) days advance written notice of non-renewal is provided.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of the State of Colorado and the municipal code of the City of Boulder.
