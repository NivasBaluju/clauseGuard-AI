1. RENT AMOUNT AND LATE PAYMENT
Monthly rent is $1,500.00 due on the first day of each month. A late fee of $10.00 per day applies if rent is not received by the 5th.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT CAP UNDER ARS 33-1321
Security deposit is $1,500.00 (capped by statute at one and one-half month's rent). Landlord shall return the deposit with an itemized accounting within fourteen (14) business days of moving out.

3. LANDLORD MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES (ARS 33-1324)
Landlord shall maintain electrical, plumbing, heating, air conditioning, and ventilation in reasonable working order, including cooling systems in summer months.

4. TWO-DAY ENTRY NOTICE (ARS 33-1343)
Except in emergencies, Landlord shall provide at least two (2) days written notice of intent to enter and enter only at reasonable times.

5. SUBLETTING RESTRICTION
Tenant may not assign or sublet the dwelling unit without Landlord's written authorization.

6. NOTICE OF TERMINATION
Either party may terminate month-to-month tenancy by giving at least thirty (30) days written notice prior to the periodic rental date.

7. LATE PAYMENT PENALTIES
Late fee of $10.00 per day applies if rent is not received by the 5th day of the month.

8. ALTERATIONS AND PAINTING
Tenant shall make no modifications, painting, or lock alterations without Landlord's written authorization.

9. TENANT INSURANCE
Tenant is strongly advised to maintain renter's insurance with at least $100,000.00 liability coverage.

10. INDEMNITY
Tenant agrees to defend and indemnify Landlord against liabilities resulting from Tenant's negligence.

11. UTILITY RESPONSIBILITY
Tenant pays electric and gas. Landlord provides water, sewer, and regular trash collection.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Parties agree to explore mediation prior to filing formal forcible detainer proceedings.

13. LEASE RENEWAL
Month-to-month extension requires thirty (30) days written notice of non-renewal.

14. PET PROVISIONS
Pets permitted with written approval and $200 pet deposit. Service animals are exempt.

15. GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the Arizona Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (ARS Title 33 Chapter 10).
