CLAUSE 1: OCCUPANCY AND RENT
Lease term starts August 1, 2024, ending July 31, 2025. Rent is $2,400.00 per month due on the 1st of each month. Late fee of $35.00 applies after the 5th of the month.

CLAUSE 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT PURSUANT TO CA CIVIL CODE 1950.5
Security deposit of $2,400.00 shall be held in trust. Landlord must offer a pre-move-out initial inspection two weeks before surrender, and return the deposit with receipts within 21 days.

CLAUSE 3: MAINTENANCE STANDARDS
Landlord shall maintain hot and cold running water, heating, and weatherproofing per California Civil Code 1941.1. Tenant shall promptly notify landlord of plumbing or roof defects.

CLAUSE 4: ENTRY RESTRICTIONS AND NOTICE
Landlord must provide 24 hours written notice before entering the unit. Notice must state the approximate time of entry during normal business hours.

CLAUSE 5: SUBLEASE RIGHTS
Tenant may sublease during the academic breaks. Landlord must approve or reject subtenants within 7 days based solely on documented financial capability.

CLAUSE 6: PET RULES
No pets allowed without signed Pet Agreement and $250.00 deposit. Service animals and psychiatric service dogs are exempt under the Americans with Disabilities Act.

CLAUSE 7: JURY TRIAL WAIVER VOID
In compliance with California public policy, neither party waives their right to a jury trial in any judicial proceeding arising from this agreement.

CLAUSE 8: TERMINATION TERMS
Tenancy terminates on the agreed expiration date. Month-to-month extension requires thirty (30) days written notice to terminate.

CLAUSE 9: LATE PAYMENT CHARGES
If rent is not received by the 5th calendar day of the month, a reasonable administrative fee of $35.00 shall be assessed.

CLAUSE 10: ALTERATIONS RESTRICTION
Tenant shall make no structural alterations, painting, or lock changes without prior written approval of Landlord.

CLAUSE 11: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is advised to maintain renter's insurance with at least $100,000.00 liability protection during tenancy.

CLAUSE 12: INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant agrees to indemnify Landlord from claims resulting from Tenant's negligent acts or omissions on the property.

CLAUSE 13: UTILITY BILLING
Tenant is responsible for PG&E gas and electric services. Landlord pays municipal water, trash, and sewer.

CLAUSE 14: LEASE RENEWAL AND EXTENSION
Upon expiration of the term, this tenancy continues month-to-month under Berkeley Rent Stabilization Ordinance provisions.

CLAUSE 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of California and the City of Berkeley Rent Stabilization Ordinance.
