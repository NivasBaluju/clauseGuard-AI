ARTICLE 1: TERM AND MONTHLY RENTAL
This lease runs for one (1) year beginning September 1, 2024. Monthly rent is $2,300.00 payable on the first day of each month.

ARTICLE 2: LATE FEE CEILING AND GRACE PERIOD
No late fee shall be charged unless rent remains unpaid for five (5) full days after the due date. The late fee shall not exceed $50.00 or 5% of monthly rent, whichever is less.

ARTICLE 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW LAWS
Security deposit is limited by law to one month's rent ($2,300.00). Landlord holds deposit in trust and will return deposit minus legitimate deductions within fourteen (14) days of tenant moving out.

ARTICLE 4: WARRANTY OF HABITABILITY (RPL 235-b)
Landlord warrants premises are fit for human habitation and free from conditions hazardous to life, health, or safety. Any lease provision waiving this warranty is void.

ARTICLE 5: INSPECTION ENTRY NOTICE
Landlord may enter premises only with reasonable advance notice of at least twenty-four (24) hours, except in case of fire or water emergency.

ARTICLE 6: SUBLEASING AND ROOM SHARING LAWS
Tenant has the legal right to share the apartment with immediate family and one additional occupant pursuant to Real Property Law Section 235-f (The Roommate Law).

ARTICLE 7: RETALIATORY EVICTION PROHIBITION
Landlord shall not retaliate against Tenant by increasing rent or terminating tenancy for exercising legal rights or reporting housing code violations under RPL 223-b.

ARTICLE 8: NOTICE OF RENEWAL OR NON-RENEWAL
Pursuant to the Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act (HSTPA), Landlord must provide 60 days advance written notice if electing not to renew this lease.

ARTICLE 9: ALTERATIONS AND FIXTURES
Tenant shall not make structural modifications, paint, or install major appliances without Landlord's advance written consent.

ARTICLE 10: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is encouraged to secure personal property insurance covering tenant belongings against casualty loss.

ARTICLE 11: INDEMNIFICATION PROVISIONS
Under General Obligations Law 5-321, any lease clause exempting landlords from liability for their own negligence is void. Tenant indemnifies landlord only for tenant's own willful acts.

ARTICLE 12: UTILITY ALLOCATION
Landlord provides heat and hot water. Tenant pays electric and cooking gas through direct utility submetering.

ARTICLE 13: PET ADDENDUM
Pets are permitted in accordance with NYC Pet Law / NY state tenant guidelines. Service animals are permitted without restriction.

ARTICLE 14: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Disputes may be addressed through the New York State Attorney General Consumer Protection Bureau mediation program.

ARTICLE 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of the State of New York and the Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act.
