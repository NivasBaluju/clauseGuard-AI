SECTION 1: RENT PAYMENT AND SENIOR CITIZEN GRACE PERIOD (NJSA 2A:42-6.1)
Rent is $1,900.00 per month due on the 1st. Under New Jersey law, senior citizens and disability pension recipients receive a mandatory 5-business-day grace period without late penalty.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW AND INTEREST (NJSA 46:8-19)
Security deposit is $1,900.00 (cannot exceed one and one-half month's rent). Landlord shall deposit funds in an interest-bearing account and pay annual interest to Tenant. Refund will occur within 30 days.

SECTION 3: HABITABILITY AND HEATING CODE
Landlord must provide heat between October 1 and May 1 maintaining a minimum temperature of 68 degrees Fahrenheit per New Jersey State Housing Code.

SECTION 4: 24-HOUR NOTICE FOR ENTRY
Landlord shall provide 24 hours advance written notice before entry for non-emergency repairs.

SECTION 5: ANTI-EVICTION ACT PROTECTIONS (NJSA 2A:18-61.1)
Tenants are protected under the New Jersey Anti-Eviction Act and may not be removed or refused lease renewal except for statutory good cause.

SECTION 6: LATE FEE AND GRACE PERIOD
Senior citizens and disability pension recipients receive a mandatory 5-business-day grace period. Late fee is $35.00 after grace period.

SECTION 7: PROPERTY ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall make no physical alterations, paint walls, or replace hardware without prior written authorization.

SECTION 8: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is advised to maintain insurance covering personal property and $100,000.00 liability.

SECTION 9: INDEMNITY ALLOCATION
Tenant indemnifies Landlord against claims caused solely by Tenant's own negligence on premises.

SECTION 10: UTILITIES
Tenant pays electricity and cooking gas. Landlord pays municipal water, sewer, and trash collection.

SECTION 11: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Parties may submit disputes to New Jersey Superior Court Special Civil Part mediation services.

SECTION 12: AUTOMATIC RENEWAL (NJSA 2A:18-61.1)
Under the Anti-Eviction Act, lease automatically renews unless landlord has statutory good cause for non-renewal.

SECTION 13: SUBLETTING TERMS
Subletting requires Landlord's written authorization which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

SECTION 14: PET COVENANT
Pets permitted with written approval and $250 pet deposit. Service dogs exempt from deposits.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey and the Truth in Renting Act (NJSA 46:8-43 et seq.).
