1. LEASE TERM AND RENT
Lease commences June 1, 2024, ending May 31, 2025. Rent is $1,400.00 per month due on the 1st. Late fee of $30.00 applies if unpaid by the 5th.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND 30-DAY REFUND
Tenant deposits $1,400.00. Landlord shall refund deposit and provide itemized damage deductions within thirty (30) days of moving out per Texas Property Code 92.103.

3. LANDLORD REPAIR OBLIGATION UNDER TPC 92.056
Landlord must make a diligent effort to repair any condition that materially affects physical health or safety of an ordinary tenant within seven (7) days of written notice.

4. ADVANCE NOTICE FOR ENTRY
Landlord shall provide 24 hours advance notice of entry for routine maintenance and safety inspections.

5. SUBLEASING PROHIBITED WITHOUT CONSENT
Tenant shall not sublease or assign the apartment without prior written authorization from property management.

6. PET DEPOSIT AND POLICIES
Pets require an approved pet agreement and $250.00 deposit. Service and emotional support animals are permitted without charge.

7. NOTICE OF TERMINATION
Either party may terminate by delivering written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the monthly period.

8. LATE FEE REGULATIONS (TEXAS PROPERTY CODE 92.019)
Late fee of $30.00 applies if rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, conforming to Texas statutory reasonableness requirements.

9. PROPERTY ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall make no alterations, painting, or physical modifications without prior written consent.

10. TENANT INSURANCE
Tenant is strongly advised to maintain a renter's insurance policy throughout tenancy.

11. INDEMNITY
Tenant agrees to defend and hold Landlord harmless against claims arising from Tenant's negligence or intentional acts.

12. UTILITY BILLING
Tenant pays electric to Austin Energy. Landlord provides water, sewer, and curbside collection.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Parties may submit disputes to the Austin Dispute Resolution Center before seeking judicial remedies.

14. LEASE RENEWAL AND EXTENSION
Lease continues month-to-month after initial term with 30 days advance written notice required to terminate.

15. GOVERNING LAW
Governed by Texas Property Code Chapter 92 and local municipal housing regulations.
