1. TERM AND RENT
Term begins August 15, 2024, and terminates August 14, 2025. Monthly rent is $1,550.00 payable on the 1st day of each month. A late charge of $30.00 applies after the 5th calendar day.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT PROTECTIONS
Tenant pays $1,550.00 security deposit. Pursuant to ATCP 134, Landlord shall provide an itemization of damages and mail the remainder within 21 days after vacating.

3. PROMISE TO REPAIR AND HABITABILITY
Landlord promises to maintain structural soundness, heating facilities capable of 67 degrees, and running potable water. Repairs will commence within 72 hours of written notification.

4. LANDLORD ACCESS RESTRICTION
Landlord shall give at least 24 hours advance notice of entry, specifying the time of entry which must occur between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM on weekdays, except for emergency repairs.

5. SUBLETTING APPROVAL
Subletting is permitted with written authorization. Landlord shall not impose an application fee on proposed subtenants exceeding $25.00.

6. PET PROVISIONS
No unauthorized pets are permitted. Unauthorized pets will result in a fee of $50.00 per occurrence. Service animals are exempt.

7. UTILITY PAYMENTS
Tenant pays electric and gas. Landlord pays municipal water, sewer, and regular recycling services.

8. NON-RENEWAL AND TERMINATION
Non-renewal notice must be given in writing at least 60 days before the lease end date.

9. LATE CHARGE PROVISIONS
A late payment fee of $30.00 will be assessed if rent is not received by 5:00 PM on the 5th day of the month.

10. ALTERATION RULES
Tenant shall not paint, refinish floors, or make structural additions without Landlord's advance written consent.

11. INSURANCE REQUIREMENT
Tenant is advised and required to secure renter's insurance covering personal liability and property loss.

12. INDEMNITY OBLIGATIONS
Tenant agrees to indemnify Landlord from claims arising solely from negligent acts committed by Tenant or Tenant's guests on the premises.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Parties may utilize the Tenant Resource Center mediation service to resolve landlord-tenant disputes informally.

14. AUTOMATIC EXTENSION AND RENEWAL
This lease expires on August 14, 2025, and requires sixty (60) days advance written notice of renewal or termination.

15. GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of the State of Wisconsin and Dane County ordinances.
