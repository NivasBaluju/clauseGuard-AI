SECTION 1: RENT AND PAYMENT DUE DATES
Monthly rent is $1,600.00 due on the first day of each calendar month. Under North Carolina General Statutes Section 42-46, a late fee may not exceed fifteen dollars ($15.00) or five percent (5%) of the monthly rent, whichever is greater, and may only be charged if rent is late by five days or more.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW UNDER NC TPAA
Tenant deposits $1,600.00 as security. In compliance with the North Carolina Tenant Security Deposit Act (NCGS 42-50), the deposit is deposited in an escrow account at Wells Fargo Bank in Chapel Hill, NC. Landlord will account for and refund the deposit within thirty (30) days of termination.

SECTION 3: HABITABILITY AND SMOKE DETECTORS
Landlord shall comply with all applicable building and housing codes and repair all electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems within a reasonable time. Landlord will install and test working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms at occupancy commencement.

SECTION 4: LANDLORD RIGHT OF ENTRY
Landlord shall provide at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice prior to entering the premises for non-emergency inspections, appraisals, or showings to prospective tenants.

SECTION 5: SUBLETTING AND ROOM ASSIGNMENT
Tenant may sublet the premises with Landlord's written approval. Landlord shall not unreasonably delay evaluation of subtenant applications.

SECTION 6: PROHIBITION ON PETS
No pets allowed on premises without an authorized pet agreement and a $200.00 refundable pet deposit. Emotional support and service animals are permitted pursuant to fair housing guidelines.

SECTION 7: UTILITIES AND ALLOCATION
Tenant pays electricity and internet. Landlord provides water, sewer, and stormwater drainage fees.

SECTION 8: NOTICE OF TERMINATION
Written notice of at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration date is required to terminate or non-renew this lease.

SECTION 9: LATE PAYMENT CHARGES
Under NCGS 42-46, a late fee of $15.00 or 5% of monthly rent shall be assessed if rent is late by five days or more.

SECTION 10: PROPERTY ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall not paint, wallpaper, or make physical alterations without Landlord's written authorization.

SECTION 11: RENTER'S INSURANCE POLICY
Tenant is required to maintain an active renter's insurance policy with $100,000.00 personal liability coverage.

SECTION 12: INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant indemnifies Landlord against claims caused by Tenant's own negligence or breach of tenancy terms.

SECTION 13: DISPUTE MEDIATION
Parties agree to seek resolution through the Orange County Dispute Settlement Center prior to initiating judicial proceedings.

SECTION 14: AUTOMATIC EXTENSION
Tenancy converts to month-to-month upon expiration unless terminated with 30 days written notice.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by North Carolina law and the North Carolina Residential Rental Agreements Act.
