CLAUSE 1: RENT TERMS AND PAYMENT
Rent is $1,250.00 per month, due on the 1st of each calendar month. A reasonable late fee of $25.00 applies if rent is unpaid after the 5th.

CLAUSE 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW (RC 5321.16)
Deposit of $1,250.00 is held in escrow. Under Ohio Revised Code 5321.16, deposits held for more than 6 months shall bear 5% interest per annum. Landlord will mail itemized deductions and refund within thirty (30) days.

CLAUSE 3: STATUTORY REPAIR OBLIGATIONS (RC 5321.07)
Landlord shall maintain electrical, plumbing, heating, and ventilating fixtures in good working condition. If Landlord fails to repair within 30 days of written notice, Tenant may escrow rent with municipal court.

CLAUSE 4: ENTRY NOTICE RESTRICTION (RC 5321.04)
Landlord must give at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice of intent to enter and enter only at reasonable times, except in emergencies.

CLAUSE 5: SUBLETTING TERMS
Tenant may not sublet without Landlord's written approval. Approved subtenants must sign an addendum.

CLAUSE 6: TERMINATION PROCEDURES
Termination of periodic tenancy requires at least thirty (30) days written notice prior to the periodic rental date.

CLAUSE 7: LATE FEE CHARGES
Late fee of $25.00 applies if rent is not received by the 5th day of the month.

CLAUSE 8: ALTERATIONS AND FIXTURES
Tenant shall not make physical alterations or drive unauthorized anchors into walls without permission.

CLAUSE 9: INSURANCE COVERAGE
Tenant is encouraged to maintain renter's insurance for personal property and liability.

CLAUSE 10: INDEMNITY
Tenant indemnifies Landlord against claims arising from Tenant's failure to maintain safe tenancy conditions.

CLAUSE 11: UTILITIES
Tenant pays gas and electric. Landlord pays municipal water, sewer, and refuse services.

CLAUSE 12: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Parties may engage in municipal court mediation prior to trial proceedings.

CLAUSE 13: AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Tenancy converts to month-to-month with 30 days notice required to end occupancy.

CLAUSE 14: PET POLICIES
Pets require written authorization and $200 deposit. Service animals are permitted without fee.

CLAUSE 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by Ohio Revised Code Chapter 5321 (Ohio Landlord-Tenant Act).
