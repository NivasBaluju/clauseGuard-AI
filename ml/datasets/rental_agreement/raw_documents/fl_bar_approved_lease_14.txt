SECTION 1: TERM AND RENT
Term begins October 1, 2024, and ends September 30, 2025. Rent is $1,950.00 per month, due on the 1st of each month. Late fee of $50.00 applies after the 4th day of the month.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT HOLDINGS UNDER FS 83.49
Tenant deposits $1,950.00 as security. Landlord shall hold the deposit in a separate Florida banking account and notify Tenant of the depository within 30 days. Deposit shall be returned within 15 days if no claim is made, or 30 days if damages are claimed.

SECTION 3: LANDLORD MAINTENANCE COVENANT
Under Florida Statutes Section 83.51, Landlord shall maintain plumbing in reasonable working condition, maintain roofs, windows, doors, and keep common areas clean and structurally sound.

SECTION 4: RIGHT OF ACCESS NOTICE (FS 83.53)
Landlord may enter the dwelling unit at any time for the protection or preservation of premises in an emergency, or upon giving at least twelve (12) hours advance notice for inspections and repairs between 7:30 AM and 8:00 PM.

SECTION 5: SUBLETTING RESTRICTION
Tenant shall not sublet or assign any interest in the premises without prior written authorization from Landlord.

SECTION 6: PET COVENANT AND DEPOSITS
No pets allowed without Landlord's written permission and a refundable deposit of $300.00. Service animals are permitted in compliance with applicable law.

SECTION 7: UTILITIES AND SERVICE CHARGES
Tenant is responsible for all electricity, internet, and cable. Landlord provides water, sewer, and regular trash collection.

SECTION 8: CASUALTY DAMAGE AND TERMINATION
If premises are substantially damaged by hurricane or fire, Tenant may immediately vacate and terminate the agreement with rent abated from the date of damage.

SECTION 9: LATE CHARGES
Rent received after the 4th day of the month shall incur a late charge of $50.00.

SECTION 10: ALTERATIONS RESTRICTION
Tenant shall make no alterations, painting, or wallpapering without prior written consent of Landlord.

SECTION 11: TENANT INSURANCE
Tenant is required to maintain a renter's insurance policy with $100,000.00 liability limit throughout tenancy.

SECTION 12: INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant agrees to indemnify Landlord from and against claims arising out of Tenant's negligent acts on the premises.

SECTION 13: MEDIATION OF DISPUTES
Prior to litigation, parties agree to attempt resolution through Florida Bar grievance mediation or local community mediation.

SECTION 14: RENEWAL NOTICE
Either party may terminate or request renewal with at least thirty (30) days written notice prior to term expiration.

SECTION 15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by Chapter 83 of the Florida Statutes (Florida Residential Landlord and Tenant Act).
