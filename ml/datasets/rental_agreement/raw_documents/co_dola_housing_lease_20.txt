1. RENT PAYMENT AND LATE FEES (HB 21-1121)
Rent is $1,850.00 per month due on the 1st. In compliance with Colorado House Bill 21-1121, late fees cannot exceed $50.00 or 5% of past due rent, whichever is greater, and may only be charged after a 7-day grace period.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND 60-DAY EXTENSION (CRS 38-12-103)
Tenant deposits $1,850.00. Landlord will return the deposit within thirty (30) days (or up to 60 days if explicitly stated) with an itemized accounting of deductions.

3. WARRANTY OF HABITABILITY REMEDIES
Landlord warrants compliance with CRS 38-12-503. Landlord must commence remedial action within 24 hours for lack of heat, water, or life-safety hazards.

4. LANDLORD ACCESS (48-HOUR ADVANCE NOTICE)
Landlord shall provide at least 48 hours notice before entering for non-emergency inspections or repair work.

5. SUBLETTING AND PROHIBITED FEES
Subletting is permitted with written authorization. Landlord cannot charge fees exceeding actual screening costs.

6. NOTICE TO VACATE
Either party may terminate month-to-month tenancy by giving at least twenty-one (21) days notice under CRS 13-40-107.

7. LATE FEE RESTRICTIONS (HB 21-1121)
Late fees cannot exceed $50.00 or 5% of past due rent and may only apply after a 7-day grace period.

8. ALTERATIONS RESTRICTION
Tenant shall make no alterations or structural modifications without Landlord's written authorization.

9. RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant is advised to maintain insurance for personal property and $100,000.00 premises liability.

10. INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant indemnifies Landlord for damages caused solely by Tenant's negligence or intentional wrongful acts.

11. UTILITY RESPONSIBILITIES
Tenant pays electricity and heating gas. Landlord provides water, sewer, and regular trash collection.

12. DISPUTE MEDIATION
Parties may submit disputes to Colorado Community Mediation Services prior to filing in county court.

13. LEASE RENEWAL AND EXPIRATION
Converts to month-to-month unless 30 days advance notice of non-renewal is delivered.

14. PET POLICIES
Pets allowed with written addendum and refundable pet deposit. Service animals exempt.

15: GOVERNING LAW
Governed by Colorado Revised Statutes Title 38 Article 12.
