SECTION 1: PERSONAL PROPERTY COVERAGE
Covers personal property owned by insured.

SECTION 2: LOSS OF USE EXPENSES
Living expenses for uninhabitable dwelling unit.

SECTION 3: INSURED PERILS
Fire, lightning, windstorm, vehicle damage, theft.

SECTION 4: EXCLUSIONS
Excludes earthquake, flood, and sewer backup.

SECTION 5: DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 standard deductible.

SECTION 6: CANCELLATION STATUTES (OCGA 33-24-44)
Requires 30 days written notice for cancellation.

7. GRACE PERIOD
30 days grace period for payment of renewal premium.

8. FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Fraudulent misrepresentation voids the policy.

9. APPRAISAL
Appraisal process applies if parties disagree on value.

10. SUBROGATION
Company subrogated to rights of recovery upon payment.

11. POLICY PERIOD
Covers direct physical loss during policy period.
