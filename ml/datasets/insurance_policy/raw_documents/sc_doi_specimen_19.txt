1. COVERAGE C
Covers personal property of insured.

2. LOSS OF USE
Reimburses living costs during repairs.

3. PERILS COVERED
Fire, lightning, wind, hail, theft, vandalism.

4. HURRICANE DEDUCTIBLE
Percentage deductible applies during named hurricane events.

5. EXCLUSIONS (WATER)
Excludes flood, storm surge, and drain backup.

6. CLAIMS NOTICE
Prompt notice required.

7. CANCELLATION
30 days advance notice required.

8. GRACE PERIOD
Thirty days grace period for renewal premium.

9. FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Fraudulent misrepresentation voids coverage.

10. APPRAISAL OF LOSS
Appraisal mechanism for valuation disputes.

11. SUBROGATION
Assignment of recovery rights upon payment.

12. POLICY PERIOD
Policy applies to loss during declared term.
