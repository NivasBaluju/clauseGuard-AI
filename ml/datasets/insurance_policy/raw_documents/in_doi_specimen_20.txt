SECTION I: PERSONAL PROPERTY
Covers personal property anywhere in the world.

SECTION I: LOSS OF USE
Covers additional living expenses.

SECTION I: PERILS
Covers fire, lightning, windstorm, explosion, riot, theft.

SECTION I: EXCLUSIONS
Excludes flood, water backup, and earth movement.

SECTION I: DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 per occurrence.

SECTION I: NON-RENEWAL NOTICE (IC 27-7-12)
Notice of non-renewal must be provided at least 20 days prior to expiration.

7. GRACE PERIOD
30 days grace period for renewal premium.

8. FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Intentional concealment voids insurance.

9. APPRAISAL
Disputes on amount of loss resolved by appraisal.

10. SUBROGATION
Subrogation rights assigned upon claim payment.

11. POLICY PERIOD
Covers loss during declared policy period.
