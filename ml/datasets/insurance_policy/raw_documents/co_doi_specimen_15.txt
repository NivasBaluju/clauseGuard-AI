1. COVERAGE C
Personal property owned or used by insured worldwide.

2. LOSS OF USE
Additional living expenses covered for up to 12 months.

3. PERILS COVERED
Fire, wind, hail, smoke, theft, vandalism.

4. EXCLUSIONS (EARTH MOVEMENT AND WATER)
Excludes landslide, mudflow, and flood.

5. DEDUCTIBLE
$1,000.00 property deductible.

6. NOTICE REQUIREMENTS
Prompt notice of damage and itemized inventory.

7. NON-RENEWAL NOTICE
Requires 30 days notice prior to expiration.

8. GRACE PERIOD
Thirty days grace period for renewal premium payment.

9. FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Intentional fraud voids policy coverage.

10. APPRAISAL PROCEDURE
Appraisal procedure for disputed claim amounts.

11. SUBROGATION
Subrogation transfer upon claim payment.

12. POLICY PERIOD
Annual policy period for covered losses.
