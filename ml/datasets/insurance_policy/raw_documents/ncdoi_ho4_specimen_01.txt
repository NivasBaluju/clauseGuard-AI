SECTION I - COVERAGE C PERSONAL PROPERTY
We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. At your request, we will cover personal property owned by guests or residence employees while on the residence premises.

SECTION I - COVERAGE D LOSS OF USE
The limit of liability for Coverage D is the total limit for additional living expenses and fair rental value necessary to maintain your normal standard of living if a covered loss makes the residence premises uninhabitable.

SECTION I - PERILS INSURED AGAINST
We insure for direct physical loss to property caused by fire or lightning, windstorm or hail, explosion, riot or civil commotion, vehicles, smoke, vandalism, and theft.

SECTION I - GENERAL EXCLUSIONS (WATER DAMAGE)
We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by water damage, meaning flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of any body of water, or water or water-borne material which backs up through sewers or drains.

SECTION I - EARTH MOVEMENT EXCLUSION
We do not insure for loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudflow, or earth sinking, rising or shifting.

SECTION I - DEDUCTIBLE
Unless otherwise noted, the standard deductible is $500.00. We will pay only that part of the total of all loss payable under Section I that exceeds the deductible amount.

SECTION I - CONDITIONS (DUTIES AFTER LOSS)
In case of a loss, you must give prompt written notice to us or our agent within sixty (60) days, notify police in case of theft, protect property from further damage, and submit a signed proof of loss.

SECTION I AND II - CANCELLATION CONDITIONS
You may cancel this policy at any time by returning it or notifying us in writing. We may cancel for non-payment upon ten (10) days notice, or for any other reason upon thirty (30) days advance notice.

SECTION I AND II - SUBROGATION WAIVER
An insured may waive in writing before a loss all rights of recovery against any person. If not waived, we may require an assignment of rights of recovery to the extent payment is made.

SECTION I AND II - DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND APPRAISAL
If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party may demand an appraisal of the loss in writing. Each party will select a competent appraiser within twenty (20) days.

SECTION I AND II - GRACE PERIOD FOR PREMIUM
A grace period of thirty (30) days will be granted for the payment of each renewal premium, during which time the policy shall remain in force.
