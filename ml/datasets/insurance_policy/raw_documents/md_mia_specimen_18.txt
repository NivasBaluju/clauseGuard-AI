SECTION I: PROPERTY COVERAGE
Covers personal property up to limit of liability.

SECTION I: ADDITIONAL LIVING EXPENSE
Reimburses reasonable living costs.

SECTION I: PERILS INSURED
Fire, smoke, wind, vehicle damage, theft.

SECTION I: WATER DAMAGE EXCLUSION
Excludes flood, surface water, and sewer backup.

SECTION I: DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 deductible.

SECTION I: PROOF OF LOSS
Must submit proof of loss within 60 days.

SECTION I: 45-DAY NON-RENEWAL NOTICE
Notice of non-renewal must be mailed 45 days in advance.

8. GRACE PERIOD

9. CONCEALMENT OR FRAUD
Material misrepresentation voids coverage.

10. APPRAISAL
Appraisal clause for disputed loss amount.

11. SUBROGATION
Subrogation rights transfer upon payment of loss.

12. POLICY PERIOD
