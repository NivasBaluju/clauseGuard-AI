1. PROPERTY COVERAGE
Coverage C covers personal property owned or used by insured worldwide.

2. LOSS OF USE
Additional living expense for uninhabitable premises.

3. PERILS COVERED
Fire, wind, hail, smoke, theft, vehicle damage.

4. EXCLUSIONS
Excludes earth movement, flood, neglect, and intentional acts.

5. DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 standard deductible per occurrence.

6. NOTICE OF LOSS
Notice required within reasonable time.

7. CANCELLATION RULES
45 days advance notice required for non-renewal under Washington law.

8. GRACE PERIOD
A grace period of 30 days is granted for payment of renewal premium.

9. CONCEALMENT OR FRAUD
Willful concealment or misrepresentation of material fact voids policy coverage.

10. APPRAISAL CLAUSE
Disputes on valuation of loss may be submitted to appraisal by neutral appraisers and umpire.

11. SUBROGATION
Company acquires right of recovery against third parties upon payment.

12. POLICY TERM
Policy applies to losses occurring during the 12-month period specified.
