SECTION 1: PERSONAL PROPERTY
Covers personal property anywhere in the world.

SECTION 2: LOSS OF USE
Covers additional living expense.

SECTION 3: PERILS INSURED
Fire, lightning, windstorm, explosion, riot, smoke, vandalism, theft.

SECTION 4: WATER EXCLUSION
Excludes sewer backup and flooding.

SECTION 5: DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 deductible per loss.

SECTION 6: CANCELLATION (MCL 500.2833)
30 days advance notice required for non-renewal.

7. GRACE PERIOD
30 days grace period for renewal premium payment.

8. FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Fraudulent statements void coverage under Michigan law.

9. APPRAISAL
Appraisal process for loss valuation disagreement.

10. SUBROGATION
Company acquires recovery rights upon payment.

11. POLICY TERM
Applies to loss occurring during effective policy period.
