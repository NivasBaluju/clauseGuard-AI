1. COVERAGE C
Covers personal property worldwide.

2. LOSS OF USE
Provides living expenses when premises uninhabitable.

3. COVERED PERILS
Fire, lightning, hail, explosion, vandalism, theft.

4. EXCLUSIONS
Excludes flood, earthquake, and freezing in unheated buildings.

5. DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 standard deductible.

6. APPRAISAL PROVISION (MINN. STAT. 65A.01)
Statutory appraisal process for valuation disputes.

7. NON-RENEWAL NOTICE
60 days advance written notice required.

8. GRACE PERIOD
Grace period of 30 days allowed for renewal premium.

9. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
Intentional fraud voids policy.

10. APPRAISAL
Appraisal procedure for property valuation disputes.

11. SUBROGATION
Subrogation assignment upon payment.

12. POLICY PERIOD
Covers direct physical loss during policy period.
