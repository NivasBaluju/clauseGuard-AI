1. COVERAGE C PROPERTY
Personal property covered anywhere in the world.

2. LOSS OF USE
Covers necessary increase in living expense.

3. BROAD PERILS
Covers 16 broad named perils including fire and theft.

4. WATER EXCLUSION
Excludes water damage from sewers or drains.

5. DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 deductible per loss.

6. APPRAISAL DISPUTES
Disputes on valuation handled by independent appraisers.

7. CANCELLATION NOTICE
Notice of non-renewal must be mailed at least 30 days in advance.

8. GRACE PERIOD FOR PREMIUM
Grace period of 30 days granted for renewal premium remittance.

9. FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Willful misrepresentation of material facts voids coverage.

10. SUBROGATION PROVISION
Subrogation rights assigned to company upon payment.

11. POLICY PERIOD
Policy applies to loss occurring during effective policy period.
