1. COVERAGE C PERSONAL PROPERTY LIMIT
We insure personal property owned by an insured up to the limit of liability stated in the declarations page.

2. LOSS OF USE LIVING EXPENSE
If a covered peril makes your rented residence uninhabitable, we cover actual reasonable additional living expenses for up to 12 months.

3. NAMED PERILS COVERAGE
Direct physical damage caused by fire, lightning, windstorm, hurricane, smoke, vandalism, and falling objects.

4. FLOOD AND WATER EXCLUSION
Loss resulting from flood, rising water, storm surge, or sewer backup is strictly excluded regardless of concurrent causes.

5. WINDSTORM DEDUCTIBLE
A separate calendar year hurricane deductible equal to 2% of Coverage C applies to losses caused by windstorm during a declared hurricane.

6. NOTICE OF LOSS TIME BAR
Initial notice of a windstorm or hurricane claim must be filed within one (1) year of the date of loss per Florida Statutes Section 627.70132.

7. CANCELLATION BY INSURER
We may cancel upon 120 days notice prior to renewal, or 10 days notice for non-payment of premium.

8. RIGHT OF SUBROGATION
We acquire all legal rights of recovery against responsible third parties upon payment of any claim.

9. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
This entire policy is void if, whether before or after a loss, an insured has willfully concealed or misrepresented any material fact.
