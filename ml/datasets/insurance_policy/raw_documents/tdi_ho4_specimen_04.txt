SECTION I - COVERAGE C (PERSONAL PROPERTY)
Provides worldwide coverage for personal property owned by an insured.

SECTION I - LOSS OF USE EXPENSES
Reimburses necessary additional living expenses if residence is untenantable after a covered loss.

SECTION I - SPECIFIED PERILS
Insures against fire, lightning, smoke, vandalism, windstorm, and theft.

SECTION I - WATER BACKUP EXCLUSION
Direct exclusion of loss resulting from water backing up from sewers, drains, or sump pumps.

SECTION I - DEDUCTIBLE TERMS
$500.00 deductible applied per covered occurrence.

SECTION I - PROMPT NOTICE AND PROOF OF LOSS
Must submit signed, sworn proof of loss within ninety (90) days of company request.

SECTION II - CANCELLATION PROVISIONS
Company may cancel upon 30 days written notice, or 10 days for non-payment.

SECTION II - SUBROGATION RIGHTS
Subrogation transfer of rights upon claim payment.

SECTION I - DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND APPRAISAL
If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party may demand an appraisal in writing.

SECTION I - GRACE PERIOD FOR PREMIUM PAYMENT
A grace period of thirty (30) days is allowed for payment of renewal premiums before coverage lapses.

SECTION I - FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Coverage is void if an insured intentionally misrepresents material facts in connection with any claim.

SECTION I - POLICY PERIOD
Applies only to losses occurring during the policy period stated in the declarations.

SECTION I - SUBROGATION ASSIGNMENT
We acquire all legal rights of recovery against third parties upon payment of loss.
