SECTION I: COVERAGE C
Covers personal property owned or used by insured.

SECTION I: LOSS OF USE
Additional living expenses incurred due to covered damage.

SECTION I: PERILS INSURED
Fire, lightning, hail, explosion, civil commotion, vandalism, theft.

SECTION I: EXCLUSIONS (FLOOD)
Loss caused by flood, waves, and surface water is excluded.

SECTION I: DEDUCTIBLE
Standard deductible of $500.00 applies.

SECTION I: CLAIMS DUTIES
Prompt notice and signed sworn proof of loss required within 60 days.

SECTION I: CANCELLATION
Requires 30 days advance notice for cancellation.

SECTION I: GRACE PERIOD
Thirty (30) days grace period allowed for renewal premium before coverage lapse.

SECTION I: FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Material misrepresentation or fraud voids all coverage under policy.

SECTION I: APPRAISAL OF LOSS
Appraisal procedure available for loss valuation disputes.

SECTION I: SUBROGATION
Company acquires subrogation rights upon claim payment.

SECTION I: POLICY TERM
Applies to loss occurring during policy period.
