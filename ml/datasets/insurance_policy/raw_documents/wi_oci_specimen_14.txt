SECTION I: PROPERTY COVERAGE
Worldwide personal property coverage for named insured.

SECTION I: ADDITIONAL LIVING EXPENSE
Reimburses reasonable living costs after covered peril.

SECTION I: PERILS INSURED
Fire, lightning, explosion, riot, smoke, theft, freezing.

SECTION I: EXCLUSIONS
Excludes flood, sewer overflow, and earth sinking.

SECTION I: DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 per occurrence.

SECTION I: NON-RENEWAL (WIS. STAT. 631.36)
Notice of non-renewal must be mailed at least 60 days prior to expiration.

7. GRACE PERIOD
30-day grace period for renewal premium.

8. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
Material misrepresentation voids coverage.

9. APPRAISAL
Independent appraisal for valuation disputes.

10. SUBROGATION
Insured transfers recovery rights to insurer upon payment.

11. POLICY PERIOD
Applies only to loss during policy period.
