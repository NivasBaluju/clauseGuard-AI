1. COVERAGE C
Covers personal property worldwide up to policy limits.

2. COVERAGE D
Loss of use covers additional living expenses.

3. NAMED PERILS
Covers fire, wind, hail, smoke, vandalism, and theft.

4. EXCLUSIONS (WATER DAMAGE)
Excludes flood and sewer backup.

5. DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 standard deductible.

6. PROOF OF LOSS
Must submit proof of loss within 60 days.

7. CANCELLATION TERMS
30 days advance notice required for non-renewal.

8. GRACE PERIOD
Grace period of 30 days granted for renewal premiums.

9. FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Concealment or fraud voids coverage for all insureds.

10. APPRAISAL OF LOSS
Disputes on loss value resolved by appraisal.

11. SUBROGATION
Assignment of recovery rights against third parties upon payment.

12. POLICY PERIOD
Covers loss during policy period stated in declarations.
