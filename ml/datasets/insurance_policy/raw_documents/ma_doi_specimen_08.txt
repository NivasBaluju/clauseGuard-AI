SECTION 1: PERSONAL PROPERTY COVERAGE
Covers personal property anywhere in the world.

SECTION 2: LOSS OF USE LIMIT
Provides up to 20% of Coverage C limit for additional living expenses.

SECTION 3: PERILS INSURED AGAINST
Fire, lightning, explosion, vandalism, theft, freezing of heating.

SECTION 4: WATER AND SEWER EXCLUSION
Direct exclusion of subsurface water and sewer backup.

SECTION 5: DEDUCTIBLE AMOUNT
$500.00 standard deductible.

SECTION 6: PROOF OF LOSS REQUIREMENT
Signed proof of loss within 60 days of request.

SECTION 7: NON-RENEWAL AND CANCELLATION
45 days advance written notice required for cancellation or non-renewal.

SECTION 8: GRACE PERIOD
A 30-day grace period is provided for payment of each renewal premium.

SECTION 9: FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Policy is void if insured intentionally conceals material facts or commits fraud.

SECTION 10: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND APPRAISAL
If parties fail to agree on amount of loss, appraisal may be demanded under Massachusetts law.

SECTION 11: SUBROGATION
Insured transfers recovery rights against responsible third parties upon payment.

SECTION 12: POLICY PERIOD
Covers direct loss occurring during the policy period.
