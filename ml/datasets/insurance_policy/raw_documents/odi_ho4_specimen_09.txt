1. COVERAGE C PERSONAL PROPERTY
Worldwide coverage for personal property owned by an insured.

2. COVERAGE D LOSS OF USE
Covers living expenses while premises are untenantable.

3. COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
Fire, lightning, windstorm, explosion, riot, smoke, theft.

4. WATER DAMAGE EXCLUSION
Excludes water backup through sewers and surface flooding.

5. DEDUCTIBLE
$500.00 per occurrence.

6. PROOF OF LOSS
Must be filed within 60 days.

7. 30-DAY NON-RENEWAL NOTICE
Notice of non-renewal must be delivered at least 30 days before expiration.

8. GRACE PERIOD
A grace period of 30 days is granted for payment of each renewal premium.

9. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
Intentional concealment or fraud in connection with a claim voids coverage.

10. APPRAISAL CLAUSE
Valuation disputes handled by independent appraisers and an umpire.

11. SUBROGATION RIGHTS
Subrogation transfer of recovery rights upon payment of loss.

12. POLICY DURATION
Policy runs for one annual term from effective date.
