SECTION I: PERSONAL PROPERTY LIMIT OF LIABILITY
We cover personal property up to the Coverage C limit shown in Declarations.

SECTION I: LOSS OF USE REIMBURSEMENT
Covers reasonable additional living expenses if property is rendered uninhabitable.

SECTION I: COVERED PROPERTY PERILS
Fire, lightning, windstorm, explosion, riot, smoke, vandalism, and theft.

SECTION I: EXCLUSIONS (FLOOD AND GROUNDWATER)
Excludes surface water, flood, and sewer backup without specialized endorsement.

SECTION I: DEDUCTIBLE REQUIREMENT
Deductible of $500.00 per occurrence.

SECTION I: DUTIES AFTER OCCURRENCE
Must submit proof of loss within sixty (60) days after insurer request under NY Insurance Law 3407.

SECTION I AND II: CANCELLATION (NYIL 3425)
Non-renewal notice must be mailed at least 45 days, but not more than 60 days, prior to expiration.

SECTION I AND II: SUBROGATION ASSIGNMENT
Company subrogated to rights of recovery against responsible parties.

SECTION I AND II: GRACE PERIOD
Thirty (30) days grace period granted for renewal premium before coverage termination.

SECTION I AND II: FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
Any intentional material misrepresentation voids coverage under New York Insurance Law.

SECTION I AND II: APPRAISAL OF LOSS
If parties fail to agree on loss amount, appraisal may be demanded pursuant to NYIL Section 3408.

SECTION I AND II: POLICY PERIOD
Covers losses occurring during the policy period stated on Declarations.
