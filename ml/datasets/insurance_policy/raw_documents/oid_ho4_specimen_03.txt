SECTION 1: PROPERTY COVERAGES
We cover personal property anywhere in the world owned by an insured, subject to policy exclusions.

SECTION 2: PERILS INSURED
Covers fire, lightning, windstorm, tornado, explosion, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, theft, and freezing of plumbing.

SECTION 3: EXCLUSIONS (POLLUTION AND MOLD)
Loss caused by fungi, wet or dry rot, mold, or bacteria is excluded unless resulting directly from covered plumbing discharge.

SECTION 4: DEDUCTIBLE APPLIED
$1,000.00 standard deductible per occurrence for all property perils.

SECTION 5: CLAIMS SETTLEMENT AND APPRAISAL
If the insured and company disagree on valuation, each chooses an appraiser and an umpire resolves disputes.

SECTION 6: NON-RENEWAL NOTICE
Notice of non-renewal will be delivered in writing at least forty-five (45) days before expiration.

SECTION 7: SUBROGATION CLAUSE
Insurer is subrogated to all claims against liable third parties.

SECTION 8: GRACE PERIOD FOR PREMIUM
A grace period of thirty (30) days will be granted for the payment of each renewal premium, during which time the policy continues in force.

SECTION 9: FRAUD AND CONCEALMENT
This entire policy is void if, whether before or after a loss, an insured has willfully concealed or misrepresented any material fact.

SECTION 10: POLICY PERIOD AND RENEWAL
This policy applies only to loss occurring during the policy period stated in the Declarations. We will offer renewal unless written notice is given.

SECTION 11: SUBROGATION RIGHTS
Insured must transfer all rights of recovery against liable third parties to company upon claim payment.
