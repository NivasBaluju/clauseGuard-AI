1. COVERAGE C PERSONAL PROPERTY
Worldwide coverage of personal property with 10% limit for secondary locations.

2. WILDFIRE EVACUATION EXPENSES (COVERAGE D)
Mandatory coverage for up to two weeks of living expenses upon civil authority evacuation due to wildfire.

3. COVERED CASUALTY PERILS
Covers direct physical damage from fire, lightning, explosion, theft, and falling objects.

4. EARTH MOVEMENT EXCLUSION (CALIFORNIA MANDATE)
Earthquake damage is excluded. Separate California Earthquake Authority (CEA) policy required.

5. DEDUCTIBLE APPLICATION
Standard deductible of $500.00 per occurrence.

6. NOTICE OF CLAIM FILING
Must furnish prompt notice of claim and complete inventory within 60 days.

7. 75-DAY NON-RENEWAL NOTICE (CIC 678)
Company must provide written notice of non-renewal at least 75 days before policy expiration.

8. SUIT AGAINST US LIMITATION
No action may be brought against company unless policy terms are complied with and commenced within one year of loss.

9. GRACE PERIOD FOR RENEWAL PREMIUM
A grace period of 30 days is provided for renewal premium payments to maintain continuous coverage.

10. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION (CIC 331)
Concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact entitles the injured party to rescind insurance.

11. SUBROGATION AND RECOVERY
Company is subrogated to the insured's right of recovery against third parties to the extent of claim payment.

12. APPRAISAL PROCEDURE
Valuation disputes may be submitted to independent appraisal upon written demand.

13. POLICY TERM AND RENEWAL
Policy runs for a term of one year from inception date and may be renewed upon timely payment.
