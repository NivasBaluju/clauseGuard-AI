Dear Alex Rivera,

Apex Technologies Inc. (the "Company") is pleased to extend this formal offer of employment for the position of Senior Distributed Systems Engineer.

SECTION 1: ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES
You will report to the Vice President of Engineering and perform duties customary to this position, including backend architecture, reliability engineering, and system scaling. Your initial primary work location will be the Company's Austin headquarters, with eligibility for hybrid telecommuting.

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION AND SALARY
Your starting annual base salary will be $185,000.00, payable in semi-monthly installments in accordance with the Company's standard payroll schedule, less standard statutory tax withholdings.

SECTION 3: ANNUAL PERFORMANCE BONUS
You will be eligible to participate in the Company's discretionary annual incentive plan, with a target bonus opportunity of 20% of your annual base salary, contingent upon individual milestone attainment and overall corporate profitability.

SECTION 4: HEALTH AND RETIREMENT BENEFITS
You will be eligible to participate in the Company's comprehensive employee benefit programs, including medical, dental, vision, life insurance, and a 401(k) savings plan with 4% employer matching, effective on the first day of the calendar month following your employment commencement date.

SECTION 5: AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
Your employment with the Company is strictly at-will. This means that either you or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without advance notice. Nothing in this letter shall be construed as a guarantee of continued employment for any specific duration.

SECTION 6: PROPRIETARY INFORMATION AND IP ASSIGNMENT
As a condition of employment, you must execute the Company's standard Employee Inventions and Confidentiality Agreement. You agree that all inventions, software code, proprietary designs, trade secrets, and patents developed by you during your employment relating to the Company's current or prospective business belong exclusively to Apex Technologies Inc.

SECTION 7: NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICITATION
During your employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of your employment for any reason, you agree not to directly or indirectly engage in, perform services for, or establish any commercial business that directly competes with the Company within the United States. Furthermore, for eighteen (18) months following termination, you shall not solicit or hire any employees or contractors of the Company.

SECTION 8: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION
Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to your employment, compensation, or this offer letter shall be resolved exclusively through final and binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its Employment Arbitration Rules in Austin, Texas. You waive any right to bring claims in a court of law or participate in a class action.

SECTION 9: START DATE AND CONTINGENCIES
Your anticipated start date is November 16, 2026. This offer is contingent upon successful completion of a standard background investigation, satisfactory reference verification, and your ability to furnish documentation verifying legal authorization to work in the United States within three (3) business days of your start date.

SECTION 10: OFFER ACCEPTANCE DEADLINE
This offer of employment shall remain open and valid until 5:00 PM Central Time on October 28, 2026, after which it will expire automatically unless extended in writing by the Company.
