RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

PARTIES AND PREMISES
This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into between Landlord, Johnathan Vance, and Tenant, Sarah Jenkins, for the real property located at 452 Elmwood Avenue, Apartment 3B, Rochester, MN 55902.

SECTION 1: RENT PAYMENT TERMS
Rent shall be payable in advance on or before the first day of each calendar month in the amount of $1,850.00. Payment shall be made via electronic tenant portal or by check delivered to Landlord's designated management address. If rent is not received by the fifth day of the month, a late fee shall be assessed.

SECTION 2: LATE FEES AND PENALTIES
If Tenant fails to pay the full monthly rent within five (5) days of the due date, Tenant shall incur an immediate late charge of $75.00, plus an additional recurring daily penalty of $15.00 for each subsequent calendar day until all delinquent rent is paid in full.

SECTION 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant agrees to deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,500.00 as a Security Deposit upon signing this Agreement. The deposit shall be held in an escrow account at First National Bank. The deposit shall be returned to Tenant within twenty-one (21) days after vacating and surrendering the premises, less lawful itemized deductions for damages beyond ordinary wear and tear.

SECTION 4: MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall maintain the structural walls, roof, plumbing, heating, and primary electrical fixtures in good operating condition. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any plumbing leaks or hazardous defects and shall keep the interior premises clean, sanitary, and free of unreasonable clutter.

SECTION 5: LANDLORD ENTRY AND ACCESS
Landlord or its authorized contractors may enter the leased premises upon providing reasonable advance written notice of at least twenty-four (24) hours for the purpose of inspection, repairs, pest control, or showing the premises to prospective purchasers. In an emergency involving imminent danger to life or property, immediate entry is permitted without notice.

SECTION 6: SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT
Tenant shall not assign this lease, sublet the premises, or grant any license to occupy any portion of the premises without obtaining the prior written consent of Landlord. Landlord shall not arbitrarily or unreasonably withhold consent for creditworthy replacement tenants.

SECTION 7: PET POLICY
No animals, dogs, cats, or pets of any kind shall be kept on the premises without Landlord's express written authorization and payment of a refundable pet deposit of $350.00. Legally certified service and assistance animals are exempt from all pet deposits and fees.

SECTION 8: UTILITIES AND SERVICE CHARGES
Tenant shall be solely responsible for setting up accounts and paying all charges for electricity, cooking gas, internet, and cable streaming. Landlord agrees to provide and pay for municipal water, sewer, and weekly trash removal services.

SECTION 9: TENANT LIABILITY INSURANCE
Tenant is required to obtain and maintain an active renter's personal property and comprehensive liability insurance policy with minimum personal liability coverage of $100,000.00 throughout the duration of the tenancy. Tenant shall provide proof of coverage within ten (10) days of moving in.

SECTION 10: INDEMNIFICATION AND HOLD HARMLESS
Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord and its property managers from any claims, suits, liabilities, costs, and reasonable attorney fees arising directly out of Tenant's negligent acts or intentional misconduct on the premises.

SECTION 11: TERMINATION AND NOTICE TO VACATE
Either party may terminate this month-to-month tenancy by serving written notice of termination to the other party at least thirty (30) days prior to the intended surrender date. If Tenant vacates prior to the expiration without statutory justification, Tenant remains liable for rent until re-rented.

SECTION 12: AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date.

SECTION 13: GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by, construed, and enforced in accordance with the laws of the State of Minnesota. Any formal legal proceedings shall be instituted in the District Court of Olmsted County.
